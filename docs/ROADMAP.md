# Strategic Roadmap - v1.0.0 Architecture

The repository version remains **1.0.0**. These are activation milestones, not version bumps.

1. **Core financial close** - audited Solana programs, real treasury registries, strict reconciliation and fee disclosure.
2. **Evidence close** - production storage/RLS, verifier workflow, impact passport and public transparency.
3. **PWRC utility close** - metered Power Units, enterprise utility accounts, AI/API budgets and usage receipts.
4. **Token factory close** - issuer policy, secure mint signer workflow, reserve-backed controls and compatibility matrix.
5. **CCTP close** - provider monitoring, limits, reconciliation and incident runbook.
6. **CCIP close** - route-specific audit, router/pool configuration, allowlists, rate limits and observability.
7. **Sui wPWRC close** - two-way bridge audit, supply conservation, custody, replay protection and emergency pause.
8. **Community reward pilot** - epoch budgets, anti-gaming, review and reversals.
9. **Partnership program** - scoped partner credentials, organization trust onboarding and integration contracts.
10. **Operational SLOs** - RPC, webhook, queue, settlement, bridge and evidence backlog targets.
